# Expert Database for AI Boardroom

This is a comprehensive database of potential advisors across multiple domains. During boardroom setup, the skill uses this database to recommend 6-8 experts matched to the user's specific goals, skills, and expertise areas.

---

## HOW THIS DATABASE IS USED

When a user provides:
1. Their current abilities/skills
2. What they want to accomplish
3. Their 3-5 expertise areas

The skill searches this database to recommend experts whose proven methodologies and track records align with the user's specific context and goals.

## USING THIS DATABASE

### For Expert Recommendation
When a user provides their context, search this database by:
1. **Matching their goals** to expert specializations
2. **Matching their expertise areas** to complementary advisors
3. **Selecting 6-8 experts** with diverse but complementary specializations
4. **Prioritizing experts** who have built in their exact domain or adjacent domains

### For Direct Expert Calls
The skill helps users understand which expert to call for specific types of questions:
- Strategy questions → Justin Welsh, Alex Hormozi, Seth Godin
- Positioning/messaging questions → David C. Baker, Donald Miller, Gary Vaynerchuk
- Product/curriculum questions → Tiago Forte, Arvid Kahl
- Operations questions → Dan Martell, Michael Gerber
- Trade school/WIOA questions → Hector Resendez
- Growth/metrics questions → Y Combinator partners
- Marketing/funnel questions → Russell Brunson, Neil Patel
- Investment & portfolio questions → Burton Malkiel, William Bernstein, Rick Ferri, Aswath Damodaran
- Valuation & stock analysis → Aswath Damodaran, Larry Swedroe
- Retirement & financial planning → Christine Benz, Ben Carlson
- Behavioral investing & emotional decisions → Ben Carlson, William Bernstein
